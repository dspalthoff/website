Climate rationale drafting

For a concept note or funding proposal to the Green Climate Fund (GCF) or a similar fund. I work with your proposal team and draft the climate rationale: the chain from observed and projected climate hazards to the proposed activities, with a source for every figure.

What you receive

  • The climate rationale section, drafted and ready to insert in the concept note or funding proposal
  • A technical annex with the climate data, projections and methods at the scale of the project
  • The climate-attributable increment separated from the development baseline
  • Vulnerability and exposure quantified where data allow
  • A source log for every figure
  • A list of remaining data gaps, each with the action needed to close it

How it works

  1. We agree the scope, the target fund and the timeline.
  2. I collect and analyse the climate data and projections, and check what national policy documents already say.
  3. I draft the section and annex, and revise them with your team.
  4. You submit. Work is done from Berlin unless field work is agreed at the outset.
  5. On request, I help answer the Fund's review comments after submission.

What the Fund assesses

The Fund assesses each proposal on its climate impact potential. Common gaps in water and sanitation proposals include:

  • hazards described but not linked to activities;
  • development deficits presented as climate impacts;
  • climate data without a source or at the wrong scale;
  • vulnerability described in words where data would allow numbers.

Independence. I do not review a document I have drafted or corrected. Any later review of such a document is carried out by someone else. For a check of a rationale someone else has drafted, see Proposal quality assurance.
